FRANCAIS ATAR - Year 12 French study app
========================================

WHAT IT IS
Flashcards with a spaced-repetition scheduler (8,000+ words and phrases sorted by theme and category), phrase bank, text types,
written-exam guide, grammar with quizzes, verb conjugator, translation drills, and an oral toolkit (question sets you can upload,
spinning wheel, mock interview). Everything runs on your own computer - no account, no internet needed.

HOW TO OPEN IT (any computer)
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (e.g. Documents).
2. Double-click  index.html  - it opens in your browser.
   Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Windows, Mac or Chromebook). Safari/Firefox also work but can't save to a folder.
3. On the welcome screen click "Choose save folder" and pick (or create) a folder such as Documents/French app data.
   From then on every change is saved there automatically as ordinary files.
   (Next time you open the app after restarting the browser it may ask you to "Reconnect" the folder - one click.)

WINDOWS ALTERNATIVE
Double-click  Start-French-App.bat  - it starts a tiny local server and saves into the "userdata" folder next to the app.

YOUR DATA
* Progress, cards you add and your oral questions/answers are saved in the folder you chose (state.json, custom-cards.json, oral.json).
* Copy that folder to move to another computer, or use Settings > Download backup / Restore backup.
* Uploaded Word (.docx) files are only read; they are not stored.
* If no folder is chosen, data lives in the browser only - download a backup now and then.

SHARING
Send this whole folder (or the .zip). It contains no personal data. Each person gets their own progress and can upload their own oral questions.

TIPS
* Study > Card order: Smart (weak cards first, most useful words first) or Random.
* Oral > Manage: upload a .docx/.txt of your questions (and your model answers) - the mock exam uses them for follow-up questions.
* The examiner voice uses your browser's text-to-speech; install a French voice in your system settings if you only hear English.
